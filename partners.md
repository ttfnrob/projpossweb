## Become a partner

If you would like to partner with us, get in touch and one of us will get back to you. We'd love to know how you like to help and what questions you have for us.

We work with:

- **Schools**, who host our projects and give us a class to work with
- **Businesses**, who fund projects or donate equipment, space, and expertise
- **Community groups**, who help us reach families and get people involved
