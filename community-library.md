# Community Library

What if children could build their own library and fill it with their own books?

We want to help a class of children become published authors, learn about libraries and bookbinding, and then build a community library outside their school.

The fun part? It will be stocked with books they wrote themselves.

## About the Project

We don't want this to end when the school bell rings. This project leaves behind something permanent: a physical library, built by children, stocked with their own stories, sitting outside the school for neighbours, families, and future students to enjoy for years to come.

Every child has a story to tell. This project takes that seriously. Children will go through the full journey of becoming a published author: writing, editing, illustrating, and seeing their words become a real bound book with their name on the cover. Then they'll build the library to house it.

## What's Involved

- **Write &amp; Create:** We work with the school to support children in writing and illustrating their own books.
- **Print &amp; Bind:** Books are printed and hand-bound. Each child sees their words become a real, physical book.
- **Build the Library:** We go into school to construct the community library together, then fill it with the children's books.

3 phases over several weeks, 2–3 volunteers needed.

**Skills covered:** Creative writing, illustration, art, editing, woodwork, upcycling, sustainability, teamwork.

**Age Group:** Age range KS2, approximately UK Years 3-6.

Project Possibility uses JustGiving as our fundraising platform.
