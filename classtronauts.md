We have flown Classtronauts twice. The first flight was with **Windmill Hill Primary School in Sheffield**,
before Project Possibility was founded as a charity. The second was **Classtronauts 2024**, with
**Priors Wood Primary School in Ware**.

## Classtronauts 2024: Priors Wood

We worked with the children at Priors Wood to define a space mission with a message for the world.
Their message.

We launched it into space with the kids at their own Mission Control, and monitored it as it rose to
30km above the Earth, broadcast their message, and then descended back to the ground.

We talked with Year 5 about the history of messages sent into space, and about what kind of messages
they would want to hear. In the end it was up to them to decide what to broadcast from the top of the
world, and they didn't disappoint.

## The full flight of Teamwork-1

We were not permitted to launch from Ware, so Teamwork-1 lifted off from south of Sheffield instead
and flew 100 miles to the Yorkshire coast. You can watch the entire 2.5 hour journey, including the
broadcast of their message at the very peak, and the dramatic landing.

## Before the charity: Windmill Hill

Our first flight was with Windmill Hill Primary School in Sheffield, and it happened before Project
Possibility was formally founded. We worked with the school to design a curriculum covering space,
communication and the search for life, then launched a balloon into the stratosphere and brought back
footage of the Earth for the children to share with their community.
