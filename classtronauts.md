We worked with the children at Priors Wood school to define a space mission with a message for the world. Their message.

We launched it into space with the kids at their own Mission Control, and monitored it as it rose to 30km above the Earth, broadcast their message, and then descended back to the ground.

We talked with Year 5 about the history of messages sent into space, and about what kind of messages they would want to hear. In the end it was up to them to decide what to broadcast from the top of the world, and they didn't disappoint.
