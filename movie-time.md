What if a class could write, film, and premiere their own movie?

We want to help children devise, cast, and film their very own movie, then hold a real premiere at a local cinema, complete with red carpet and popcorn. The whole community is invited.

## About the Project

One week. That's all it takes. Children will write the script, design the sets, perform in front of the camera, and see the whole thing come together as a finished film. Then comes the big night, a genuine premiere screening at a local cinema or film venue, with their families and community in the audience.

This isn't about producing a perfect film. It's about giving children the experience of collaborating under pressure, making creative decisions, and seeing something through from a blank page to a packed screening room.

## What's Involved

- **Write &amp; Plan:** Children devise the story, write the script, and plan the production. Casting roles both in front of and behind the camera.
- **Film &amp; Create:** Sets are designed and built, scenes are filmed, and the movie is edited together. Everyone has a role: actors, set designers, directors.
- **Premiere Night:** The finished film is screened at a local cinema or venue. Red carpet, popcorn, and the community is invited to watch.

Approx. one week of filming, with the premiere held separately. 2–3 volunteers needed.

**Skills covered:** Creative writing, drama and performance, A/V filming and editing, set design and art, teamwork, project planning.

**Age Group:** Age range KS2, approximately UK Years 3-6.
