We want to help children devise, cast, and film their very own movie, then hold a real premiere at a local cinema, complete with red carpet and popcorn. The whole community is invited.

## About the project

One week. That's all it takes. Children write the script, design the sets, perform in front of the camera, and see the whole thing come together as a finished film. Then comes the big night: a genuine premiere screening at a local cinema, with their families and community in the audience.

This isn't about producing a perfect film. It's about giving children the experience of collaborating under pressure, making creative decisions, and seeing something through from a blank page to a packed screening room.

## What's involved

- **Write and plan:** Children devise the story, write the script, and plan the production, casting roles both in front of and behind the camera.
- **Film and create:** Sets are designed and built, scenes are filmed, and the movie is edited together. Everyone has a role: actors, set designers, directors.
- **Premiere night:** The finished film is screened at a local cinema or venue. Red carpet, popcorn, and the community invited to watch.

Roughly one week of filming, with the premiere held separately. Two to three volunteers needed.

**Skills covered:** Creative writing, drama and performance, filming and editing, set design and art, teamwork, project planning.

**Age group:** KS2, roughly UK Years 3 to 6.
