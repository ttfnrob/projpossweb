Supporters pushed their limits for Project Possibility at Ware Lido: walking barefoot across burning coals, plunging into an ice-cold swim, or taking on both challenges back to back.

It was never really about the money, though we did need it. It was about proving to yourself that the impossible is possible when you put your mind to it.

## The two challenges

<div class="two-col" markdown="1">
<div markdown="1">
### 🔥 The fire walk

Walking barefoot across a bed of burning hot coals, guided by experienced facilitators. Equal parts mental challenge and physical achievement.
</div>
<div markdown="1">
### ❄️ The ice swim

Taking the plunge into the icy water at Ware Lido. Cold water swimming has real health benefits, but mostly it just takes courage.
</div>
</div>
