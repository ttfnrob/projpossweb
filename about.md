We organise inspiring events that promote a healthy belief in the impossible, through science, technology, and the arts.

The idea is simple. If a child gets to do something genuinely extraordinary, something they were fairly sure was impossible, it changes what they think they're capable of. That belief tends to stick around.

## How we work

We partner with schools and community groups, and we build the project with the children rather than delivering it to them. They make the decisions: what the message says, what the story is, what gets broadcast.

Everything we run is free for the children taking part. That's what the fundraising is for.
